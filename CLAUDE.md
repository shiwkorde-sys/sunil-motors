# Sunil Motors – instructions for Claude

## Design work (always)
For ANY UI, page, component, dashboard, banner or visual design in this repo, first use the **ui-ux-pro-max** skill (`.claude/skills/ui-ux-pro-max`). Also use `ui-styling` and `design-system` when relevant.

House style, from the owner (Shiwank, a product designer who does not code):
- Minimal, modern, Apple-inspired. Shades of blue only for the brand colour (accent #0071e3). No warm/copper themes.
- Calm cool-gray surfaces, white rounded cards, soft shadows, generous spacing, uniform cards, no horizontal rules in stats.
- Numbers use tabular figures; SKUs on one line.
- Add small, meaningful interaction animations (hover lift, press scale, sliding selection), and respect reduced motion.
- Mechanic phone app (`/m`): big touch targets, Voice is the one primary action.

## Working rules
- Explain in simple language. Use minimal credits.
- Always ask for confirmation before changing production (Supabase or merging to `main`).
